[flat, matter-of-fact] Suitcases have wheels. Nobody asks why.
[pause]
[flat] The answer begins in 1970.
[pause]
[flat] Before that, luggage was simply carried. By hand. [slight pause] Forever.
[pause]
[deadpan] Everyone agreed this was fine. [beat] It was not fine.
[pause]
[flat] A man looked at a suitcase. [slight pause] Then he looked at a wheel.
[pause]
[deadpan] It took him twenty years to connect these two objects.
[pause]
[flat] We now call this genius. At the time, people were furious.
[pause]
[deadpan] The wheels worked perfectly. [slight pause] Until they met a kerb.
[pause]
[flat] So we invented a solution. [beat] You pick it up and carry it.
[pause]
[deadpan] Fifty years of progress. We are back where we started.
